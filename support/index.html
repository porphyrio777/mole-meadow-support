How to play

Tap brown, golden, gift, or boss moles when they appear. Rabbits, hamsters, and hedgehogs are friendly, so let them pass. The 18-stage adventure introduces score goals, carrot defense, Mole King battles, and changing patterns. Endless and Daily Challenge modes are available from the home screen.

Audio and haptics

Open the audio/settings button to control music, sound effects, and haptic feedback separately. If the app is silent, also check the device volume and Silent Mode settings.

Progress

Stage progress, achievements, high scores, and preferences are stored only on this device. Mole Meadow does not currently provide cloud sync or progress transfer. Deleting the app also deletes its local progress.

Connectivity and purchases

The game is free, works offline, and does not require an account, purchases, or subscriptions. Build 9 has no ads or reserved ad stripe. Limited pseudonymous gameplay analytics use the network, but analytics failures do not prevent playing. Pauses, menus, results, and background time are excluded from active-play metrics.

如何游玩

地鼠出现时，点击棕色地鼠、金色地鼠、礼物地鼠或地鼠王。兔子、仓鼠和刺猬是动物朋友，请不要点击它们。18 个闯关关卡会加入得分目标、胡萝卜保卫战、地鼠王挑战和不同出场规则。主页还提供无限挑战和每日挑战。

音乐、音效与触觉反馈

打开音频/设置按钮，可以分别开关背景音乐、游戏音效和触觉反馈。如果没有声音，也请检查设备音量和静音模式。

进度记录

关卡进度、成就、最高分和偏好设置只保存在当前设备。目前不支持云端同步或进度转移。删除 App 也会删除本地进度。

网络与付费

游戏免费下载，可以离线运行，不需要账号、App 内购买或订阅。Build 9 没有广告，也没有预留广告空条。有限的化名化游戏分析使用网络，但分析网络失败不影响游玩。暂停、菜单、结果页和后台时间不计入有效游玩统计。
