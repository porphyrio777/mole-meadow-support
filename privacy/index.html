Mole Meadow

Privacy Policy

Effective for analytics-enabled builds and last updated: August 13, 2026

This policy applies to all five app languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Spanish. 简体中文 is available below.

Summary

Mole Meadow collects limited, pseudonymous gameplay analytics to understand level difficulty and improve the game. It does not sell data or track users across apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

The app stores gameplay progress, achievements, high scores, difficulty selection, anonymous analytics attempt counters, and audio or haptic preferences locally on your device. These local values provide the game experience and help label anonymous gameplay events.

Analytics information

When analytics is enabled, the app sends level start, completion, failure, explicit exit, restart, and adaptive-assistance events to PostHog EU Cloud. Events may include the stage number, game mode, difficulty, attempt number, broad duration and score ranges, device category, app language, and app version or build. PostHog also generates an anonymous identifier for this app installation.

The app does not send exact tap history, exact scores, exact play duration, names, email addresses, ages, account information, advertising identifiers, precise location, or free-form user content. IP geolocation enrichment, person profiles, automatic interaction capture, and session replay are disabled. Analytics is used only to understand aggregate gameplay and improve level balance. It is not linked to an account and is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. See PostHog’s privacy information for details about its service.

Accounts, network services, and third parties

The app does not require or provide an account. PostHog EU Cloud is the only analytics service used. The app does not use advertising networks, third-party login, cloud sync, or session replay. It does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, health data, or other sensitive device data. Gameplay remains available offline; analytics delivery requires a network connection and does not affect gameplay.

Children and families

Mole Meadow is a general-audience, family-friendly game. The app does not ask for a child’s name, contact information, age, account, advertising identifier, or precise location. Its limited analytics is not used for advertising or tracking.

Data deletion

You can delete locally stored game data and the app’s local analytics identifier by deleting the app from your device. There is no server-side player account. Historical analytics events are pseudonymous and are not connected to a name or contact address. Privacy questions can be sent to the contact below.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to team.emitu@gmail.com.

隐私政策

适用于启用分析功能的版本；最后更新日期：2026 年 8 月 13 日

摘要

《开心打地鼠》会收集有限的化名化游戏分析数据，用于了解关卡难度并改进游戏。App 不会出售数据，也不会跨 App 或网站跟踪用户。

保存在设备上的信息

App 会在设备本地保存游戏进度、成就、最高分、难度选择、匿名分析尝试次数以及音频或触觉偏好。这些本地数据用于提供游戏体验，并为匿名游戏事件添加必要标签。

分析信息

启用分析功能后，App 会向 PostHog 欧盟云发送关卡开始、通关、失败、主动退出、重试以及触发动态辅助等事件。事件可能包括关卡编号、游戏模式、难度、尝试次数、宽泛的时长和分数区间、设备类别、App 语言以及版本或构建号。PostHog 还会为本次 App 安装生成匿名标识符。

App 不会发送精确点击记录、精确分数、精确游戏时长、姓名、电子邮箱、年龄、账号信息、广告标识符、精确位置或用户自由输入内容。IP 地理位置补充、用户档案、自动交互采集和会话回放均已关闭。分析数据仅用于了解总体游戏体验和调整关卡，不与用户账号关联，也不用于广告或跨 App 跟踪。有关该服务的详情，请参阅 PostHog 隐私信息。

账号、网络服务与第三方

App 不需要且不提供用户账号，PostHog 欧盟云是唯一使用的分析服务。App 不使用广告网络、第三方登录、云同步或会话回放，也不请求访问通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风、精确位置、健康数据或其他敏感设备数据。游戏本身仍可离线游玩；分析数据传输需要网络连接，但不会影响游戏。

儿童与家庭

《开心打地鼠》是一款适合家庭体验的大众游戏。App 不会要求儿童提供姓名、联系方式、年龄、账号、广告标识符或精确位置；有限的分析数据不用于广告或跟踪。

删除数据

从设备删除 App 即可删除本地游戏数据和本地分析标识符。App 不提供服务器端玩家账号。历史分析事件采用化名标识，不与姓名或联系方式关联。如有隐私疑问，可通过下方方式联系。

政策变更

如果 App 的数据处理方式发生变化，本政策与 App Store 隐私披露会在变更后的版本发布前更新。

联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，请发邮件至 team.emitu@gmail.com。
