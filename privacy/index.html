Mole Meadow

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0, Build 8; updated: October 11, 2026

This policy applies to all five app languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Spanish. 简体中文 is available below.

Summary

Mole Meadow uses limited, pseudonymous gameplay analytics to understand level difficulty and approximate active playtime. Build 8 also includes Google’s official test banner ads. Gameplay analytics is separate from advertising and is not used for cross-app tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app stores gameplay progress, achievements, high scores, difficulty selection, anonymous analytics attempt counters, and audio or haptic preferences locally on your device. It also saves a random play-session identifier and partial active-time checkpoint to recover an interrupted session without counting offline time.

Analytics information

The app sends Adventure level start, completion, failure, explicit home exit, restart, assistance, pause, and resume events to PostHog EU Cloud. Properties include stage, campaign size, difficulty, attempt number, prior-clear status, broad score/duration ranges, broad pause reasons, device category, language, version, and build.

For Adventure, Endless, and Daily Challenge, Build 8 also sends play-session start, cumulative active-time checkpoints, and session-end events. A random session identifier, cumulative active time rounded down to 15-second increments, broad time ranges, and an interruption indicator accompany these events. Pauses, ads, home, results, and background time are excluded. Counts represent anonymous installations, not verified people; the same person can use multiple devices and multiple people can share a device.

PostHog generates an anonymous installation identifier. Explicit gameplay events do not include names, email, age, accounts, advertising identifiers, precise location, exact scores, exact duration, raw tap history, or free-form content. IP geolocation enrichment, person profiles, automatic interaction capture, and session replay are disabled. These metrics are not sent to AdMob or linked to a player account. See PostHog’s privacy information.

Test advertising and consent

Build 8 displays a small Google Mobile Ads test banner below the playfield during play, pause, and results. Official demo IDs are used; these banners earn no revenue. Test ads still contact Google and must not be treated as no data collection.

Google’s SDK may handle IP address and inferred general location, device identifiers, diagnostics/performance data, advertising data, and ad interactions. Our requests are non-personalized, publisher first-party identification/personalization is disabled, and ad content is limited to G. The app does not request ATT authorization or explicitly access IDFA. Non-personalized advertising does not mean no collection. See Google’s SDK disclosure and Google’s privacy policy.

Real AdMob IDs, monetized ads, and production consent are not enabled in Build 8. The implementation requires Google UMP permission to request production ads and provides privacy options when UMP requires them; production consent and withdrawal still need verification before any monetized release.

Accounts, network services, and third parties

The app does not require an account, third-party login, or cloud sync. PostHog EU Cloud provides gameplay analytics; Google Mobile Ads and UMP provide the separate advertising/consent integration. The app does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or health data. Gameplay remains available offline. Network failures in analytics or ads do not prevent playing.

Children and families

Mole Meadow is a general-audience game, including adults, and is not configured for Apple’s Kids Category. The game does not ask players for their name, contact information, age, or account. Its gameplay analytics is not used for advertising. A G ad-content limit is not a child-directed designation.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes local progress and preferences; it does not automatically remove events already transmitted to third-party services. There is no server-side player account. Historical gameplay events use pseudonymous identifiers rather than a name or contact address. Contact us with privacy questions without including sensitive personal information.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to team.emitu@gmail.com.

隐私政策

1.0 版本 Build 8；更新日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日

摘要

《开心打地鼠》使用有限的化名化游戏分析数据，了解关卡难度和大致有效游玩时长。Build 8 还包含 Google 官方测试 Banner。游戏分析与广告独立，不用于跨 App 跟踪。

保存在设备上的信息

App 在本地保存进度、成就、最高分、难度、匿名分析尝试次数以及音频或触觉偏好，也保存随机游玩会话标识和有效时长检查点，以恢复中断会话，不把离线时间计入游玩。

分析信息

App 向 PostHog 欧盟云发送闯关开始、通关、失败、主动回首页、重试、辅助、暂停和继续事件。属性包括关卡、总关数、难度、尝试次数、是否已通关、宽泛分数和时长区间、暂停原因类别、设备类别、语言、版本和构建号。

Build 8 对闯关、无限和每日挑战还记录会话开始、累计有效时长检查点和结束事件，使用随机会话标识、向下取整到 15 秒的累计时长、时长区间及中断标记。暂停、广告、首页、结果页和后台时间不计入。人数代表匿名安装，而非准确真人数量；同一人可能有多台设备，多人也可能共用设备。

PostHog 生成匿名安装标识。显式游戏事件不包含姓名、邮箱、年龄、账号、广告标识符、精确位置、精确分数或时长、原始点击记录或自由输入内容。IP 地理位置补充、用户档案、自动交互采集和会话录制已关闭，指标不传给 AdMob、不与玩家账号关联。参阅 PostHog 隐私信息。

测试广告与同意管理

Build 8 在游戏、暂停和结果页的场地下方显示小型 Google 测试 Banner，使用官方演示 ID，不产生收入。测试广告仍会连接 Google，不等于没有数据收集。

Google SDK 可能处理 IP 和推断的大致位置、设备标识、诊断与性能数据、广告数据及广告交互。请求为非个性化，发布商第一方标识与个性化已关闭，广告内容限 G 级。App 不请求 ATT 授权、不显式访问 IDFA。非个性化广告不等于不收集数据。参阅 Google SDK 披露及 Google 隐私政策。

Build 8 未启用真实广告 ID、广告收益或正式同意流程。正式广告实现要求 UMP 允许广告请求，并在需要时提供隐私选项；盈利版本启用前仍需验证真实项目的同意和撤回流程。

账号、网络服务与第三方

App 不需要账号、第三方登录或云同步。PostHog 欧盟云提供游戏分析，Google Mobile Ads 和 UMP 提供独立的广告与同意管理接入。App 不请求访问通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风、精确位置或健康数据。游戏可以离线运行，广告或分析网络失败不会阻止游玩。

儿童与家庭

《开心打地鼠》面向大众，包括成年人，目前未配置 Apple 儿童类别。游戏不询问玩家姓名、联系方式、年龄或账号。游戏分析不用于广告；G 级广告内容限制不代表面向儿童的声明。

删除数据

删除 App 会移除本地进度和偏好，但不会自动删除已传给第三方服务的事件。App 没有服务器端玩家账号。历史游戏事件使用化名标识，不使用姓名或联系方式。如有隐私问题请联系我们，并避免提供敏感个人信息。

政策变更

如果 App 的数据处理方式发生变化，本政策与 App Store 隐私披露会在变更后的版本发布前更新。

联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，请发邮件至 team.emitu@gmail.com。
